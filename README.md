One sentence, one star: an alien tourist reviews planet Earth and still
cannot stop praising the food. A judge model compares the reviews two at a
time; the funniest, most convincingly alien line that lands its food
compliment takes the $2 reward.

## The line

> One star: Earth's gravity snapped my third spine, humans queue an hour
> for burnt bean water, yet one Naples margherita made all four of my eyes
> weep with joy.

Concrete and specific (a snapped third spine, an hour-long queue, a Naples
margherita), in a baffled outsider's voice — humans queue for what the
visitor can only describe as burnt bean water — with one clear twist: the
same planet that broke his spine made all four of his eyes weep with joy
over a pizza.

## Files

- `main.py` — the packed entrypoint (built with `makefun compress` from
  `line_src.py`, 332 bytes, at most 4,096 bytes). Run it and it writes
  `/output/entry.txt`.
- `line_src.py` — the readable source of the entry.
- `LICENSE` — MIT.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

The output is deterministic: every run writes the same line (158 characters,
within the 1–200 character gate, one sentence).
